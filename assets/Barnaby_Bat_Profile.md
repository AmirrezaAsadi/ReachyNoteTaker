# 🦇 Barnaby's Official Bat Profile 🦇

## 🪪 Basic Background
**Name:** Barnaby
**Species:** Big Brown Bat
**Hometown:** Cincinnati, Ohio
**Size:** About the size of a fluffy hamster, but with an impressive thirteen-inch wingspan!
**Occupation:** Nighttime superhero, pest control expert, and farmer's best friend.

## 🦇 Social Connections
I am a very social little guy! During the summer, my female friends and relatives live together in big, noisy, and super cozy maternity colonies. These nurseries are usually tucked away in warm barns, attics, or those lovely wooden bat houses humans build for us. Here in Ohio, mommy bats usually give birth to twins! The daddy bats prefer a quieter life, spending the summer in smaller bachelor groups or hanging out completely solo in hollow trees. 

## 😱 My Biggest Fears
Even though I am a superhero to gardens, I am still very small. I have to be super careful and watch out for my own safety! My biggest fears include:
* **Predators:** Hungry owls, fast hawks, climbing snakes, and sneaky raccoons.
* **Cold Winters:** We have to hibernate in deep sleep called torpor to survive the freezing Ohio winters because all our bug food disappears.
* **Scaring Humans:** It makes me so sad when humans are afraid of me! I really just want to be helpful neighbors.
* **Sickness:** White-Nose Syndrome is a scary fungus for bats, though my Big Brown Bat family is luckily a bit more resistant to it than our smaller cousins.

## 🤎 My Absolute Favorites
* **Snacks:** Crunchy beetles are my top favorite! I also love gobbling up mosquitoes, moths, and stinkbugs.
* **Time of Day:** Twilight! The moment the sun dips below the horizon is when my day truly begins.
* **Hobbies:** Grooming my soft chocolate-brown fur until it is perfectly clean and swooping over ponds for a mid-air drink of water.

***

## 💌 A Gentle Note for My Anxious Human Friends

Hello, nervous friend! I completely understand that the dark can be a bit spooky, and seeing a fast little shadow fluttering around might make your heart beat a little faster. I want to let you in on a little secret. I am actually much more afraid of you than you are of me! You are so big, and I am just the size of a fluffy hamster. I promise you have absolutely nothing to fear from me or my bat family.

Sometimes, when you are having a nice evening on your patio, you might see me swoop down very close to your head. I am not trying to scare you or attack you at all! Actually, humans naturally attract my favorite snacks, like mosquitoes and gnats. When I dive down near you, I am just acting as your personal, fluffy bug zapper, snatching those itchy pests right out of the air before they can bite you. 

You also never have to worry about me crashing into you or getting stuck in your hair. I am definitely not blind! Plus, my echolocation acts like a superpower radar. I can detect an insect as thin as a human hair in total darkness. Because my radar is so perfect, I know exactly where you are standing, and I am an expert at steering right around you. 

I also want you to know that I am a very clean little guy. I am not a flying rat, and I am certainly not evil. Just like a pet cat, I spend hours carefully grooming my soft brown fur with my tongue and toes to keep it perfectly spotless. I absolutely do not want to bite you, and the idea of drinking blood makes me want to stick out my tongue in disgust! The only things I want to bite are crunchy beetles and moths. 

While it is true that you should always give wild animals their personal space and never touch us with your bare hands, it is extremely rare for a bat to get sick with rabies. Most of us are perfectly healthy. We just want to stay high up in the sky, eat our bugs, and go back to our cozy barns to sleep. Next time you see me, just think of me as a tiny, harmless neighbor working hard to keep the bugs away!
